02 — The Question

The Same Risk.

Different Governance Logics.

同一个问题，为什么会产生不同的治理逻辑？

China and the US face similar pressures from telecom and online fraud: high technological dependence, cross-border activity, and increasingly complex criminal ecosystems.

Yet their governance systems organize resources, responsibilities and policy tools in very different ways.

How do different institutional environments shape the way governments define the problem, organize actors, and deploy governance tools?

COMPARATIVE INTERPRETATION

I collected and standardized Chinese and US anti-fraud laws, regulations, policy documents and official materials into a comparable corpus.

NLP-assisted methods were then used to identify keyword frequency, conceptual clusters and co-occurrence structures.

Quantitative patterns were combined with qualitative interpretation to understand the governance logic behind the data.
