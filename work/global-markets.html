03 — Indonesia × Nickel

Indonesia × Nickel

RESEARCH PROPOSAL · 2024

When a Resource Becomes a Strategic Market

Indonesia holds the world's largest nickel reserves — a critical mineral for electric vehicle batteries. In recent years, the government has moved from exporting raw ore to building domestic processing capacity, reshaping global supply chains.

Policy Direction

Figures cited in the original 2024 research proposal.

This research proposal examines how Indonesia's nickel industry is transforming from a resource extraction model to a strategic market position.

The study focuses on three dimensions: the role of industrial policy in driving downstreaming, the competitive dynamics between state-owned and private actors, and the geopolitical implications for China, the US, and other major players.

06 — China × US × Indonesia

China × US × Indonesia

One Resource. Different Strategies.

CHINA

Vertical Integration

Investment → Processing → Battery Ecosystem

Entering Indonesia's nickel processing and new energy supply chain through overseas investment and industrial chain development, increasing participation in the value chain.

UNITED STATES

Supply Chain Diversification

Policy → Rules → Diversification

Securing more reliable critical mineral supply through domestic policy, market access rules, international standards and cooperation, and friend-shoring.

INDONESIA

Industrial Downstreaming

Resource → Policy → Industrial Upgrading

Leveraging nickel resource advantages, driving domestic processing through export restrictions, and further developing battery and EV industries.
