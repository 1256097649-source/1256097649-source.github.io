06 — Human Judgment

HUMAN JUDGMENT

Where AI Stops, I Start.

01

CONTEXT

AI sees data. I understand the situation behind the data — the politics, the timing, the unspoken constraints.

02

PRIORITY

AI ranks by relevance. I decide what actually matters given my goals, my values, and my current position.

03

VALIDATION

AI generates. I verify — checking logic, cross-referencing sources, and testing whether the output holds up in reality.

04

DECISION

AI informs. I decide — taking responsibility for the final call, because the consequence is mine.

“The tool changes. The thinking stays mine.”

ADAPTIVE

I adjust my approach based on what the work demands — knowing when to go deep alone, and when AI accelerates the path.
